# Plans and Tiers

## Free plan
Tiers: free
The Free plan includes one workspace, 3 projects, and community forum support only. There is no live chat or phone support. Upgrading to Pro takes effect immediately and is prorated.

## Pro plan
Tiers: pro
The Pro plan includes unlimited projects, 10 seats, live chat support with a 4 business hour response target, and monthly billing. Seats can be added at any time and are billed on the next invoice.

## Enterprise plan
Tiers: enterprise
Enterprise includes SSO, audit logs, a dedicated account manager, 24/7 support with a 1 hour response target for high priority tickets, and annual contracts.

## Downgrades and cancellations
Tiers: pro, enterprise
Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Cancelling does not delete data for 60 days. Enterprise cancellations must go through the account manager.
