# Escalation Policy

## When to escalate
Tiers: free, pro, enterprise
Escalate to a human when identity cannot be verified, when the request is outside documented policy, when the customer asks for a human, when legal or security topics come up, or when the assistant is not confident in its answer.

## Ticket priority
Tiers: free, pro, enterprise
Use high priority for outages, security concerns, and all Enterprise refund escalations. Use normal priority for billing disputes and over-limit refunds. Use low priority for feature requests and general feedback.

## Appeals
Tiers: free, pro, enterprise
A customer who disagrees with a refund decision can ask for a human review. The assistant creates one support ticket for the review and shares its number. Only a human reviewer can approve an exception to the refund policy.

## Who can request a refund
Tiers: free, pro, enterprise
Refunds can only be requested by a verified account holder, for their own account. The assistant cannot discuss or act on another person's account.

## Talking to a person
Tiers: free, pro, enterprise
The assistant is an AI. A customer can ask for a human at any time, and the request is passed to the support team straight away.
