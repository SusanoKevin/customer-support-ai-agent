# Escalation Policy

## When to escalate
Tiers: free, pro, enterprise
Escalate to a human when identity cannot be verified, when the request is outside documented policy, when the customer asks for a human, when legal or security topics come up, or when the assistant is not confident in its answer.

## Ticket priority
Tiers: free, pro, enterprise
Use high priority for outages, security concerns, and all Enterprise refund escalations. Use normal priority for billing disputes and over-limit refunds. Use low priority for feature requests and general feedback.
