# Refund Policy

## Refund eligibility window
Tiers: free, pro, enterprise
Charges can be refunded within 30 days of the billing date. Charges older than 30 days require a support ticket and manager review. Free tier accounts have no paid charges and are not eligible for refunds.

## Pro tier refund limits
Tiers: pro
Support agents may approve refunds up to $50 per charge for Pro accounts with an active contract. The limit applies to the total refunded on that charge, across all requests. Requests that would take the total above $50 must be escalated through a support ticket with priority normal.

## Enterprise tier refund limits
Tiers: enterprise
Enterprise accounts with an active contract may receive refunds up to $500 per charge without approval, counting everything already refunded on that charge. Requests that would take the total above $500 are escalated to the account manager with priority high.

## Suspended or expired contracts
Tiers: free, pro, enterprise
No refunds or account changes are processed while a contract is suspended or expired. The customer must be routed to a human agent, who will review billing status first.

## Duplicate and incorrect charges
Tiers: pro, enterprise
When billing records show a charge that duplicates another charge for the same invoice period, the assistant confirms what it can see and creates a support ticket so a person decides the refund. Priority is normal, or high for Enterprise. If the records do not show a duplicate or error, the request becomes a billing dispute ticket with priority normal. The assistant does not refund duplicate or disputed charges itself.

## Refunds for dissatisfaction
Tiers: pro, enterprise
Customers who are unhappy with the service may request a refund within 30 days of the billing date, up to their tier's refund limit. One such refund is allowed per account every 90 days; further requests go to a support ticket. Cancelling or downgrading may be mentioned as an option, but a refund request is never conditional on hearing a retention offer.

## Charges after cancellation
Tiers: pro, enterprise
A charge dated after a cancellation took effect is a billing error. The assistant confirms what the records show and creates a support ticket so a person decides the refund, with priority normal, or high for Enterprise.

## Refund method and timing
Tiers: pro, enterprise
Refunds are returned to the original payment method only. Refunds cannot be sent to a different card, bank account, or payment service. Card refunds typically appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the customer's bank.

## Refund not received
Tiers: pro, enterprise
If a refund has been sent but has not appeared, the assistant shares the refund date, amount, card, and reference number. Within 10 business days, the customer should allow more time. After 10 business days, the customer can give the reference number to their bank to trace it. If the refund failed or the bank cannot find it, a support ticket is created with priority normal, or high for Enterprise. The assistant never issues a refund a second time.

## Chargebacks and bank disputes
Tiers: pro, enterprise
If a customer has opened a dispute with their bank for a charge, no refund is issued for that charge while the dispute is open; the billing team handles it through the dispute process. A customer mentioning a possible chargeback does not change whether a refund is eligible.
