# Refund Policy

## Refund eligibility window
Tiers: free, pro, enterprise
Charges can be refunded within 30 days of the billing date. Charges older than 30 days require a support ticket and manager review. Free tier accounts have no paid charges and are not eligible for refunds.

## Pro tier refund limits
Tiers: pro
Support agents may approve refunds up to $50 per request for Pro accounts with an active contract. Requests above $50 must be escalated through a support ticket with priority normal.

## Enterprise tier refund limits
Tiers: enterprise
Enterprise accounts with an active contract may receive refunds up to $500 per request without approval. Requests above $500 are escalated to the account manager with priority high.

## Suspended or expired contracts
Tiers: free, pro, enterprise
No refunds or account changes are processed while a contract is suspended or expired. The customer must be routed to a human agent, who will review billing status first.
